About

A pharmacist who moved to the dry lab.

I trained as a pharmacist at DIPSAR, spent three years designing and photographing for student societies, then took an M.Tech at IIT Guwahati in regenerative medicine and ended up writing pipelines for cell-therapy target discovery. The pharmacy training is the part I use most: it is what keeps me asking whether a result could survive contact with a patient.
